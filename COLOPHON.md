# Colophon

This book is written in Markdown and built with
[content-kit](https://github.com/alpibrusl/content-kit), an open-source
toolchain for books-as-code. The manuscript lives in a repository with its
full history — [alpibrusl/prompt-to-ledger](https://github.com/alpibrusl/prompt-to-ledger)
— where a term-continuity linter checks that no word is used before it is
defined, and the same source renders to EPUB, HTML and PDF.

It is the third in a series of four. [*Prompt to
Production*](https://github.com/alpibrusl/prompt-to-production) is software
engineering discipline for people who cannot read code; [*Prompt to
Evidence*](https://github.com/alpibrusl/prompt-to-evidence) is analytical
discipline for people who are not mathematicians; this one is financial
discipline for people who are not accountants; [*Prompt to
Decision*](https://github.com/alpibrusl/prompt-to-decision), the fourth,
sits above all three — deciding whether to act on a recommendation, not
just verifying it. Same tooling, same discipline, four different gaps
between what an agent can now produce and the judgement its output assumes
you already have.
